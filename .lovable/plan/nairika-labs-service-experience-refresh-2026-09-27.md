# Nairika Labs service experience refresh

## What will change
- Remove the moving capabilities strip directly below the opening section.
- Replace the current service rows with five clear service cards: System Development, Web Development, IT Consultation, UI/UX Design, and Cloud & DevOps.
- Add a dedicated page for each service with benefits, deliverables, process, and a clear path to the contact form.
- Rewrite the company introduction around dependable support, partnership, practical expertise, and long-term value rather than team size.
- Redesign the working-process section as a cleaner, more engaging sequence with stronger hierarchy.
- Add a detailed contact form section and allow service pages to arrive with the relevant service preselected.
- Rebuild the footer with distinct brand, navigation, services, contact, and legal areas.

## Navigation and content
- Keep the focused one-page home experience while service details use dedicated, shareable URLs.
- Service card buttons will use accessible internal navigation and each service page will include its own metadata.
- The shared header and footer will link consistently across the home and service-detail pages.

## Technical details
- Use a dynamic `/services/$serviceId` route backed by a single service content source.
- Preserve the existing warm coral, acid-lime, charcoal, and cream visual system.
- Keep all visual values in the shared design tokens and verify desktop and mobile layouts.
